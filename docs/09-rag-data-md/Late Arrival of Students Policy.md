Subject: Late Arrival - Policy
1. General. Discipline is the hallmark of any successful and thriving
institution/ organisation. Without imposition of strict discipline the institutions perish.
Even our own religion lays a lot of emphasis on a disciplined way of life both in
theory and practice. In Punjab Daanish Schools and Centres of Excellence Authority
it assumes added importance owing to the fact that the strata from where the
students are Inducted are not at all familiar to the world of a disciplined life.
Moreover, in order to ensure their contemporary grooming in all facets of education
and training discipline has not only been made part of the curriculum but also
induced through a mix of stick and carrot approach.
2. Purpose. To conform to the rules and regulations of PDS&CEA in all
facets of education and training so as to ensure efficient management of schools by
inculcating importance of timings, regularity, punctuality and discipline in parents,
students and staff. Main purpose of this policy is to abstain students from returning
late to the school from exeats and other holidays.
3. Scope. The policy shall be implemented in all Daanish and COE Schools
with immediate effect so as to minimize late arrival from exeat, absenteeism and
encourage punctuality.
4. Execution
a. General Guidelines

(1) Exeats and other holidays will be observed according to the
academic calendar or as notified by PDS&CE Authority
Secretariat.
