Subject: Leave Policy — Boarders
1. General. The boarding houses are abodes for the young boys and girls who during
their stay at the school pursue their personal, social and intellectual growth while being away
from their homes. Living and sharing day-to-day life, the boarders develop close acquaintance
with the school staff and students in a different surrounding. While Daanish Schools
encourage the spirit that keeps the boarders closely bonded at school, we believe in keeping
their ties with their families alive by permitting the boarders to visit their homes under a well-
regulated system. This system, defined by the Leave Policy, delineates the procedures and
responsibilities involved in the processing of leave efficaciously. These will, therefore, be
implemented in letter and spirit.
2. Aim
a. To define individual tasks and leave processing routes in different situations.
b. To simplify and ensure economy in time and effort for the schools and Head
Office.
3. General Guidelines
a. Basic Conditions
(1) Leave will always be granted with the prior permission of the Principal
and in his absence by the DHM in all cases.
(2) HM/AHM is fully responsible to verify all antecedents of the
authorized person including those of the parents/ guardians before
handing over the leave certificate / child.
(3) At least one Boarding Staff members must be present in each House at
all times during the weekdays and two on weekends or
Sundays/Holidays.
(4) When granted leave by the Principal/ DHM during the school/weekdays,
the boarder will only be called from the class by them.

16. Gate Securlty

trained in the important task of scrutiny of the visitors. Also, that they are

masala etc).
d. Any school personnel, especially Gate security, is forbidden from receiving any
gifts from the visitors.
