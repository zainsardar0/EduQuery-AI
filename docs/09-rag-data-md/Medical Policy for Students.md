MEDICAL POLICY FOR STUDENTS
1. General. Daanish Schools are catering to the educational and residential needs
of a large number of students in both boy and girl campuses. Catering to health and hygiene
needs of these students hailing from extremely poor back ground is a major challenge, since
understanding and application of hygiene and disease prevention is universally low across
the disadvantaged sections of society. This policy lays the guidelines for all Daanish schools
under Daanish Authority to keep their students and staff healthier and address the medical
issues of students.
2. Principle. It must be borne in mind that 'Healthy bodies bear healthy minds. The
8 basic principle employed in the schools will be that 'Prevention is Better than Cure".
3. Aims & Objectives. Following are major aims and objectives of the policy.
Implementing this policy will provide students and staff:
a. Basic awareness about diseases through primary health education
b. Disease free, protected and safe environment
C. Healthy and hygienic environment
d. Immediate response, care and medical treatment for sick students
e. Good standard of health and hygiene.
4. Health Education - Prevention of Diseases. Since illness causes loss in health,
academic performance, resources, etc, an important aim in schools should be educating
students about basic heaith and first aid. Following shall be ensured:-
a. Students shall be provided hygienic, safe and clean environment.
b.
8 C. Students shall know importance of personal hygiene (cleanliness of body, Classrooms and dorms/hostels shall be airy and properly ventilated.
hands, feet, hair, nails, eyes, nose, ears and all sensitive areas, etc).
d. Students shall wash their hands with soap before and after meals
e. Students shall be trained to brush their teeth before breakfast and after supper.
f. Washrooms, generally the haven of diseases, shall be made especially clean.
g. Students shall not share their towels, socks, vest, undergarments, nail cutter,
tooth brush, trimmers, etc. with other students.
h. Use of razor or blade is strictly Banned in school. Only personal trimmer can be
used if need be but these would never be shared with anyone.
A meticulous 'Health Record' shall be maintained by the Doctor on each
student of the school.

7. Treatment of Diseases. Sick students shall be treated immediately. A prompt
response by all concerned (mentioned under role of different appointments) will ensure
safety of students during illness. The School Doctor shall be overall responsible for the
healthcare of the students. Medical staff will remain available on duty during office hours
and on call during after-office hours. Principals will ensure that the medical staff is
accommodated close to the dispensary. Accurate and prompt response of medical staff will
save students from minor and major life threats due to diseases and accidents. All school

8. General Mode of Treatment/Diagnosis of Diseases. Foliowing will be the mode
of treatment in Daanish Schools:-
a. Common diseases (such as influenza, diarrhea, malaria, typhoid) shall be
treated by the School Doctor in school dispensary (Ml Room).
b. The doctor may refer students for preliminary diagnosis / tests to the nearest
Government Hospital, THQ, DHQ or Division hospital after seeking permission
from the Principal.
C. In case of non-availability of facility or urgent need of reports required, he/ she
may send students for clinical tests from private clinic / lab after the permission
of the Principal
8 d. In case a facility is not available in the nearest Government hospital and nature
of disease/accident demands immediate treatment and tests of student,
medical doctor can send student to some private hospital / recognized private
medical practitioners/doctor after approval of the Principal. For this purpose, the
Principals are authorized to spend a maximum of Rs. 20,000/- per child for one-
time treatment of a student for any particular disease. Students may be referred
to Govt. hospitals located in major cities in câse of acute medical problems
(say, when medical facilities are not available in nearby locality/city).
e. School transport can be used for sick students.
f. School medical staff will accompany the students to local, tehsil, district and
divisional hospitals as and when required. In case of parents of students are
available, the Principal on advice of the doctor will decide whether the school
medical staff will accompany the student.
8 g. Parents of students shall be immediately informed about any disease carried by
his/her child, which is contagious, serious or prolonged.
9. Treatment of Serious Diseases
a. Serious diseases shall be treated as 'Emergency'. Infected student shall be
immediately shifted to the nearest Government hospital.
b. The selection of the hospital shall be based upon condition of the student,
distance of the hospital, nature and level of the disease, facilities available at
the hospital and levei of threat to life. Life-threatening diseases include heart
diseases/stroke, Chronic pulmonary disease, lower respiratory infections,
cancers of all kinds, HIV AIDS, Diabetes Mellitus, Hepatitus B & C, etc).

10. Treatment of Contagious & Infectious Diseases. Owing to the disadvantaged
background of the students, exposure to contagious diseases during vacation cannot be
ruled out. The risks of a disease spreading in a residential institution are both likely and
8 grave. Therefore, extreme precautions must be employed to prevent spread of a disease.
Following actions will be taken by the school administration upon suspecting / discovery of a
student infected with contagious/infectious disease:-
a. Immediate test from the nearest recognized laboratory/hospital.
b. In the meanwhile, the housemaster will ensure the following
(1) The affected student will be immediately quarantined to prevent the
spread of disease to other students/staff.
(2) Alf his personal items and utensils in his/her use as well as daily
consumable items like towels, toothpastes, clothes, etc shall be kept
separate.
(3) Separate arrangements will be made for him/her to dine.
(4) The clothing of the student shall be separated and washed with
detergents/Dettol etc.
8 (5) All his belongings will be kept in open for exposure to the sun daily.
(6) His cupboard will be thoroughly cleaned and disinfected.
C. Upon verification of the infection:-
(1) The student will be immediately sent on Sick Leave for treatment.
(2) His parents will be called and advised by the Principal about the school's
support in the treatment of the student (covering transport, stay,
medicines, etc used during treatment up to Rs 20,000/-). In case the
confirmation of disease through test comes during exam time, maximum
caution will be exercised for quarantine. Prior coordination will be made
with parents/guardians to send the student on Sick Leave on the day of
the last exam after he finished attempting his/her paper.

13. Provision of Medicines / Equipment
a. The school doctor shall be solely responsible for availability of necessary life-
saving medicines (including anti-venom for rabies or dog-bite, and
snake/scorpion-sting). Date of expiry of medicines is to be checked every time
before use by the doctor.
b. The school doctor will be responsible for availability of all necessary first-aid
and related valid medicines in the dispensary at all times.
C. The doctor will process a written request on a Demand Register seven days
8 before the end of the stock of medicines to the Head of Purchase Committee.
d. After approval by the Head of Purchase Committee, the register will be
forwarded to the Principal, who will mark it to Admin Officer for
procurement/purchase of medicines and provide them to the doctor.
e. The timely provision of medicines to the doctor will be treated by Admin Officer
as 'Top Priority'.
f. The doctor will satisfy himself with the quality/validity of the medicines.
g. A similar process will be followed for procurement of other medical/related
equipment for dispensary. Ensuring the quality. of such items will be the
responsibility of the school doctor.
