ACADEMICS
PROMOTION / DISCHARGE POLICY
1. Aim: The Promotion / Discharge Policy has following aims: -
a. To set the academic benchmarks and improve the schools' academic
standing.
b. To keep foremost focus on academics, it being the most assured
element towards raising the students' employability profile.
2. General Instructions
a. A student has to pass all subjects to be promoted.
b. All failures to be given a chance to resit the exams. No student
will be allowed to repeat any Class more than once in a
student's stay at school. Students can only repeat Class VI or
VII once after which the case for discharge will be forwarded
to Academics for approval of MD/CEO.
3. Class - VI
a. Passing criteria
(1) Minimum 40% in all subiects (core and Non-core).
(2) Minimum 50% on the aggregate.
(3) Conditions:
a) Student who fails in core or Non - core subiects
will be given a special chance to pass the failed
papers. The Special Chance Exam will be held
within a fortnight (14x days) of the last paper of
final exam. The student will have to fulfill the
passing criteria, otherwise he will be considered
failed.
b) If a student is unable to appear in any / all subjects in
the final exam due to an emergency (medical issue /
death in the family, etc). he/she will be qiven a chance at
the earliest possible time to appear in the missed papers
but no later than 30 days into the next session. The
student will have to clear all subiects with a minimum
passing criteria in a single attempt.
Core - Subiects: Math, English, G. Science
Non-Core Subjects: S.Studies, Islamiat, Urdu,
Computer, Arts
4. Class-VII
a. Passing criteria
(1) Minimum 40% in each individual subject.

5. Class-VIII
a. Students obtaining an aggregate less than 55% in PEC and
Internal combined exam will be discharged.
6. Matric
a. Class - IX: The rules of BISE will be applicable. There will be no
discharge or detention in Class IX even if the student fails in all the
subjects. Students who fail any subject in Class X will be rusticated
after Class - X exams. They will not be promoted to Class - XI.
b. Students who obtain less than 65% aggregate marks in Matric will be
discharged.
C. Students will be allowed to take pre-medical or pre-engineering
streams only if they score 70% or more marks in Matric.
7. This policy supersedes all previous policies.
