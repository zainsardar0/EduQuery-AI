DAANISH INDUCTION POLICY
1. Purpose. To ensure effective centralized system of induction into Daanish
Schools, this SOP is developed according to the selection criteria and induction
quidelines as mentioned in PDS - CEA Act 2010.
2. Timeline
a. To accomplish the Daanish induction procedure, in line with the timeline
issued by the Authority (Annexure - A). Delay in any step of the
procedure may cause delay in starting the new academic sesslon.
b. The Advertisement would be printed by the mid of November or by the
20th of November.
C. The last date for receiving applications is mid-December or 20th of
December.
d. The written test will be held on the last Sunday of December
e. The provincial test will be held two weeks after the Punjab test in the
Provincial capitals of the Federating Units.
3. Procedure
a. Admission Committee. An admission committee is constituted at every
school, comprising of the following members: -
(1) Principal (Head)
(2) Deputy Head
(3) Counseling Officer
(4) Teachers (02)
(5) Other support staff on required basis
The admission committee will be the competent authority to select
capable / deserving students for Daanish Schools. After due review and
subsequent selection of forms; conducting of tests; conducting interview
of top 150 only; consolidation of results; a consolidated list of all
students will be forwarded to the Authority, who will then approve 150
students for antecedent verification through the MD / CEO. The MD /
CEO can spot check at any stage for transparency and request for data
which will be responded to immediately, when asked.
All application forms are required to be uploaded on the SMS
immediately. There will be no excuses accepted.
No rights of changing the documents or categories is given to any
school. Applications for this will be forwarded to the Academics and on
approval of the Manager, the IT Department will make the required
changes.

(3) His / Her both parents are illiterate, and do not own any
immovable property, and either parent is disabled.
(4) He / She has been a school dropout and both parents are illiterate
and the parents do not own any immovable property.

village worker, and the parents do not own any immovable
property and are illiterate.
(7) He / She is a resident of a town, tehsil or District Headquarters
and the family resides in a mud house.

Subject: Admission of Students Belonging to Deprived Economic
Background in Daanish Schools. (Established by the
Government of Puniab)
1. A number of initiatives have been taken by the Government of Punjab for
poverty reduction. Among these is the vision of poverty alleviation through provision
of quality education.
2. With this objective, Daanish Schools and Centers of Excellence Authority Act
was passed by the Provincial Assembly in 2010, paving way for the establishment of
Daanish Schools and Centers of Excellence. The focus of Daanish enterprise is on
the poorest sections of the society to spur upward social mobility and address
poverty alleviation and ensure that talent, whatever its background, does not go
unrewarded. Daanish Schools are residential schools providing free quality
education, boarding, messing, clothing, sports and co-curricular facilities to the
students from underprivileged families whose collective income is less than Rs.
15,000/- per month. The ultimate aim is to give a platform to these talented
children to realize their potential and become a useful member of society in future.
Daanish Schools have successfully entered the ninth year of their existence. These
schools (eight each for Boys & Girls) are located at Chishtian, Hasilpur, Rahim Yar
