Subject: SCHOOL INSTRUCTIONS IN FURTHERANCE OF THE QUOTA
POLICY, STUDENT/DISCHARGE DISCIPLINE POLICY
1. The ideal strength of the new entry in between 70-80 students (including all
categories). The mandatory attendance is 90 percentage.
2. Quota Seats
a. Self-finance and Provincial Quota Seats which remain vacant will not be
filled with Deserving Students.
b. Cases of self-finance students who have not paid their dues for consecutive
2 months will be processed for discharge immediately. The 2-month limit will be
taken as a benchmark for future. All cases will be processed with prior approval of
Head-Office through Academics from MD/CEO.
C. Requests for students' transfer for (quota) or deserving students will not be
processed/ entertained.
3. Building a Disciplined Outfit.
a. The school administration's lenient response towards late-comers from
leave has had a very negative effect on the overall discipline of the school.
This will no longer be the case.
(1). Class-VI students who failed to report before summer vacation may
not be called. Their admission would stand cancelled.
(2) If Class-VI students who joined before summer vacation do not
rejoin, they will not be informed again nor will anybody from school
be sent to their homes to convince them to come. However, the
provincial students will be called in writing and on phone as they
were not called before school closed for summer vacations. Please
ensure the students are touched base with and given the date to
join. If they do not join on the given date they shall be dealt with as
the deserving category. In line with the Leave Policy, their cases will
be processed for discharge to Head Office. Upon their discharge, the
seats falling vacant will not be filled by candidates next on merit.

B
(3) Students of other grades will also be dealt with in line with the
provisions of Leave Policy and no flexibility will be shown in this
respect. Their cases also be processed for discharge to Head Office
on completion of the stipulated period of absence, as stated in the
policy.
b. To bolster the discipline regime at the school, the presence of errant and
undisciplined students will no longer be tolerated. Upon first noticeable
indiscretion, the School Discipline Committee will be ordered/ assembled to
recommend their withdrawal to the Head Office.
C. As instructed earlier, zero-tolerance will be shown towards students who
involve themselves in damage to the school property.
d. Poor Attendance: Less than *90%* (instead of 75%) attendance in a term
would lead to the discharge from school.
4. Implementation of Policies.
a. The Student Discharge/Promotion Policy will be followed in true letter and
spirit, and low-achievers expelled accordingly. The policy will be made more
stringent in near future and the new provisions will be followed religiously.
It will be ensured that consistent under-achievers are weeded out before
they reach upper grades.
b. Policy about Staff Quota to be followed by the book.
5. The students with medical/health issues may be terminated on medical grounds
(Hepatitis B and C, Epilepsy, Acute Asthma, etc.) after processing the case to Head Office
with a report by RMO with recommendation for withdrawal.
6. Students with adjustments problem may be allowed to leave the school on respect
upon approval from Head Office.
7. The parents who request for withdrawal of their child from School may be
entertained and their request may be processed through Head Office. Only the students
scoring 80% and above marks in Annual Examination may be convinced to retain their
child at School.
8. Kindly acknowledge please and also follow instructions to the last dictum please.
