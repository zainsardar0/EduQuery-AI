DAY SCHOLARS - ADMISSION POLICY
0
1. Preamble: Daanish Schools started its operation in 2010 as a free residential
institute, offering free quality education to the deserving categories, who have talent, but, no
resources to study ahead. Daanish Schools provide the best possible learning opportunity to
the resource constrained children of the country. The framework of our policy stands on a
pillar which supports the building of their intellectual capacity and personality traits while
preparing their minds and bodies at the same time. To ease the exchequer of the Government,
it has been envisaged to financially support the projects by having Day Scholars at comparable
fee structures. These students will be in addition to the self-financed students and would
provide an opportunity for outreach and facilitate the local residents.
2. Aims: To build Daanish School environment, and extend its quality education to
encompass day scholars, by facilitating the smooth induction of day scholars admitted, on
nominal payment basis in Class 6, 7 and 8 only. Since this was not the precedent previously,
it requires a policy to streamline and effectively implement the induction. This would also aim
to somewhat make the schools self-financing.
3. Scope: Applicable in all Daanish Schools and for elaboration of the details involved in
the induction phase of Day Scholars from Class VI to Class VIII.
4. Execution: All Daanish Schools will start induction of maximum number of Day
Scholars from Class VI – Class VIII only according to the prescribed seats by the Authority.
a. Each school will locally advertise; propagate extensively in schools and nearby
villages for admissions.
b. The schools will start the process in January, and gather the applications.
c. Each school will be allowed to induct initially 10 students individually in Classes
6, 7 and 8. This will make 30 students as Day Scholars for the initial year.
d. Once the momentum of the programme picks up, the entry limit can be increased
on a yearly basis.
e. Each school will devise a collective entry test paper incorporating all subjects and
giving due weightage according to the Induction test paper. The papers will be
forwarded to the Academics branch no later than end of January of each year.
f. Entry tests will be conducted in Mid-March on the day announced each year.
g. New session will commence with regular classes in April of each year.
h. The Principal will forward the entrance test marks and retain details of grades
from the last school attended and the school leaving certificate or an NOC of the
school.

5. Admission: -
a. All non-residential lower staff and non-residential teaching staff's children can sit
for the Day Scholar's entry test and will be admitted only on merit. 3x seats in
each class will be reserved for them i.e. within the seats allocated: -
(1) 3x students allowed in Grade VI.
(2) 3x students allowed in Grade VII.
(3) 3x students allowed in Grade VIII.
b. Siblings of students who have passed out on whichever category can apply and
be admitted on merit.
C. Siblings of teachers or relatives of any staff may be allowed to get admissions
on merit with all rules applying to them.
6. Schedule of Payment:-
a. Schedule of payment of Day Scholars fee will be charged on a monthly basis.
Fee will be due on the 1stof every month.
b. Students are required to clear the monthly fee by the 15th of every month. In
case of non-payment, a fine of Rs. 50/- per day will be charged on intimation to
the parents.
C. The student is required to clear the dues and fine by the closure of the month,
otherwise the student will be suspended. During suspension, the student will be

EXAMPLE
1. This is only an example and not to be taken literally. It is to make it clear to you the
details.
2. If the fee of a Day Scholar was due on 1st November, and is not paid till the 15th of
November; the student continues to study in school for the month of November Rs 50/- per
day surcharge as fine. This must be conveyed to the parents or quardians. If after the 20th or
after 16th the money is given, the accounts would the fine from 16th onwards the surcharge to
the days over the 15th of November.
3. If fee is not received till the 30th or closure of the month, the student is put on immediate
suspension by the Principal. During suspension the Day scholar will not be allowed to attend
school
4. The account officer would need to vigilantly give in writing to the Principal by the 28thof
the month that the fee is still unpaid. The parents will be informed through the Principal, not to
send the Day Scholar to school and Guards would be informed not to allow the student to enter
the school unless fee is cleared.
5.The parent will be allowed 15 days after suspension. i.e., till the 15thof December to
come to the school and clear their dues and fine. If on 16th December, the parent comes to pay
then the student can rejoin classes. If not then the Principal will move the case for rustication
to Academics Department, PDS&CEA, on approval of the COO or the MD/CEO.
6. The parent would have the right to readmit the child till 3 months, i.e., till February 28th,
after paying all dues and fine. The Principal will move the case with the Account Officer's
clearance certificate, application of the parent and on approval of the Academics Department,
the student will be readmitted.
